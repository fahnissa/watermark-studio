    Good to know

    • Everything happens in your browser. Files are never uploaded.
    • Best results keep the original format (PDF stays PDF, Word stays Word, PowerPoint stays PowerPoint).
    • Converting Word or PowerPoint to PDF redraws the pages, so complex layouts may shift. For a perfect PDF, export from Word/PowerPoint first and then watermark the PDF here.
    • PDF → Word can either keep the look (pages become pictures) or keep editable text (layout is lost). No browser tool can do both perfectly.
    • Old .doc and .ppt files can't be read in a browser. Save them as .docx / .pptx first.
    • "Behind content" works on images, slides and PDFs without a solid white background. Many PDFs paint a white background, which hides anything behind it.
